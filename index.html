ABOUT MOM INTERNATIONAL
|
MOM international is a trusted supplier of motorcycle aftermarket parts
specializing in spare parts for 100cc-150cc utility motorcycles. Based in
Kolkata, India, we are focused on delivering reliable, high-quality and
competitively priced components to international markets, with a special
focus on Africa.

Our product range includes essential motorcycle components such as
brake shoes, clutch plate sets, air filters, clutch cables, throttle cables, brake
levers, chain and sprocket kits, and complete service kits
